The thermometer says one thing. Humidity and wind change what your body gets.

About these numbers

Heat index uses the NWS Rothfusz regression with its published adjustments; it assumes shade and light wind, and direct sun can add up to about 15°F. Wind chill uses the 2001 NWS formula and applies to air at or below 50°F with wind above 3 mph. Both are for healthy adults, and are guidance, not medical advice.
